Sufiyat with Yashraaj

A YASHRAAJ KAPIL LIVE IP · SUFI x BOLLYWOOD FUSION · QAWWALI & SHAYARI

Experience Mumbai's most sensational Bolly-Sufi-Qawwali fusion project — 'Sufiyat With Yashraaj' — led by Indian Idol and Sur Kshetra finalist, The Mesmerizer Yashraaj Kapil, a classically trained playback singer, composer & livewire performer whose voice can be heard in numerous Bollywood blockbusters.

This live music extravaganza blends timeless Sufi-Qawwali with just the right amount of Bollywood & modern soundscape, featuring a band of skilled musicians and classically trained backing vocalists — from traditional Qawwalis to Bollywood Sufi hits and heartfelt shayaris, there's something for everyone.
