About Us

Every successful artist has a story. Ours began on stage.

After years of performing across the globe, competing on national television, recording for Bollywood films, and entertaining audiences at thousands of live events, we learned one simple truth—talent alone doesn't build a career. It takes the right strategy, opportunities, relationships, and guidance.

That's why we founded Flair Artists & Entertainment Pvt. Ltd.

Founded by Tanu & Yashraj Kapil, Flair is a boutique talent management and entertainment company built by artists, for artists. We use our experience to discover, nurture, and manage exceptional talent, helping artists build strong brands, unlock new opportunities, and create lasting careers.

We don't just represent artists. We help them grow.
