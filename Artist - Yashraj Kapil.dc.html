Yashraaj Kapil

PLAYBACK SINGER · COMPOSER · LIVE-WIRE PERFORMER

One name. Every celebration.
From goosebumps to packed dance floors — one artist does it all.

Recognized as one of India's most dynamic, versatile and electrifying live performers, Yashraaj Kapil has built a reputation for delivering world-class live entertainment — effortlessly adapting every performance to the audience, the occasion and the energy of the moment.

From being the voice behind Bollywood hits across Khiladi 786, Policegiri and 1920: Evil Returns, to a top-finalist run on Indian Idol Season 5 and Sur-Kshetra, he pairs powerful vocals and commanding stage presence with an exceptional ability to connect with every audience.

Rather than a single act, Yashraaj offers a portfolio of distinctive live experiences — each with its own sound, personality, energy and atmosphere, curated for different celebrations and audiences.
